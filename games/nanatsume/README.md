# 七つ目の記憶 ─ 夕霧高校 旧校舎

1-bit dithered first-person horror adventure with an Obra Dinn–style monochrome look,
set in a Japanese school ghost story à la *Twilight Syndrome*.

- `index.html` — open it in a browser to play (no build step, no dependencies).
- `game.html` — the same game as a page body; `build.sh` regenerates `index.html` from it.

## How to play

A night in 1999. You sneak into the old school building to cover its seven ghost stories for the
school paper. Walk both wings with a flashlight, talk to the teacher on night duty and to the ghosts
left behind by a night in 1979, and work through a chain of small puzzles: borrow the staff-room
key, find the key-box code chalked on a 20-year-old blackboard, recover the science-room key from the
infirmary, open a dial lock, fit a red safelight bulb and develop a roll of film, and more. Six
rumors lead to the seventh: a sealed classroom that no longer appears on any floor plan.

Expect scares: faces in windows, a figure behind you, the anatomical model creeping closer, a
flickering flashlight, voices from the PA. The piano in the music room plays the opening of
Beethoven's "Moonlight" Sonata (public domain).

Keys: W/S or ↑/↓ move (hold Shift to run), A/D strafe, ←/→ or drag to turn, R/F look up/down,
E/Space inspect or talk, N notebook, 1–9 pick a dialogue choice. Touch controls appear on phones.
